# ADR-001: RiceSearcher pillar and the three-pillar boundary

**Status:** ACCEPTED — ratified in decision-challenge and confirmed against live RiceClipper/RicePoster code (2026-09-02). Q4 amended 2026-09-20 after live handoff use; downstream decisions are promoted to `SPEC.md` (D1–D8).
**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Deciders:** Finn (maintainer)

## Context

The Rice harness has two pillars today: **RicePoster** (posting automation) and
**RiceClipper** (editing / visual rendering). The current bottleneck sits *before*
both of them — finding good source material, and choosing which slice of it to
clip. This ADR introduces a third pillar, **RiceSearcher**, to own content
sourcing, and pins the responsibility boundaries across all three pillars on the
road to **L4** (automate everything up to a human approval gate; no fully
autonomous posting).

Two forces shape the decision:

- **The bottleneck is really two problems.** *Discovery* (which source material is
  worth touching) and *extraction* (which slice to cut) automate very differently
  and have different feasibility ceilings. They are treated as separate axes.
- **A two-celebrity beat has finite daily supply.** The target is **3 unique pieces
  per day (~90/month)** — stories repost the posts, so it is 3, not 6. Real-time
  supply is lumpy, so the design favours a buffered, deduplicated **library** over
  a just-in-time feed.

> **Verification constraint.** This ADR was produced without access to the live
> RiceClipper / RicePoster code. It is deliberately pitched at the level of
> *contracts crossing the pillar boundary*, not any pillar's internals. Anything
> touching RiceClipper's or RicePoster's internal behaviour is flagged below as an
> assumption to confirm before it hardens into spec.

## Decision

Introduce **RiceSearcher** as the content-sourcing pillar. Split responsibilities
along a **trim | crop** seam:

| Pillar | Owns | Produces |
|--------|------|----------|
| **RiceSearcher** | Discovery (on-demand pull) + extraction (*time*: trim boundaries) | Scored candidate slices in a deduplicated library |
| **RiceClipper** | *Space + style*: crop + render (captions / header / music) | Finished rendered clips |
| **RicePoster** | Posting | Live posts (unchanged) |

RiceSearcher owns **what/when** to clip; RiceClipper owns **how it looks**;
RicePoster posts. On the road to L4, everything up to a human **select-and-approve
gate** between RiceSearcher and RiceClipper is automated, alongside the existing
never-auto-post gate before RicePoster.

## Options Considered

Five sub-decisions were pressure-tested. Each lists the settled choice and the
alternatives it beat.

### Q1 — Where cropping lives → **trim in Searcher, crop in Clipper**

| Option | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **(a) Searcher trims, Clipper crops + renders** ✅ | Clean seam; framing logic stays in one pillar |
| (b) Searcher trims + crops, Clipper only burns captions/header/music | Splits framing across two pillars |
| (c) Searcher suggests a rough crop, Clipper refines | Middle ground; still duplicates framing intent |

**Rationale:** cropping is a visual/framing decision, and RiceClipper is the
visual pillar. Trim, by contrast, is coupled to the transcript scoring RiceSearcher
already owns. The natural seam is **trim | crop**, not trim+crop | render.

> ⚠️ **Load-bearing assumption (confirm first):** this rests on RiceClipper already
> owning blur-pad / aspect handling, so crop consolidates there cleanly. That is
> *inferred from the ratified RiceClipper spec, not verified against current code.*
> Confirm against live RiceClipper before this becomes spec.
>
> ✅ **Verified 2026-09-02 against live RiceClipper code** (`render/geometry.py`,
> `SPEC.md` D2/D12). RiceClipper **blur-pads** any non-9:16 input to 1080×1920 and
> *never crops*; true active-speaker reframe / landscape support is a deferred
> "separate project." **Maintainer ratification:** blur-pad **is** the accepted 9:16
> normalization for v1 — "crop lives in Clipper" is satisfied by blur-pad today, and
> true reframe is reinvestigated in a later wave. The trim | crop seam holds as
> written. **Historical handoff caveat (resolved by 2026-09-20):** at the time of
> the original ratification, Clipper had no pickup/import path and its manifest
> assumed already-cut clips. The delivered Searcher pickup now reads the schema-1
> filesystem handoff, imports the exact reviewed files unchanged, and independently
> transcribes them; the contract mirrors the proven mechanism, not Clipper's old
> output schema. See Q4 and `SPEC.md` §7.

### Q2 — Discovery mode → **on-demand pull in v1**

| Option | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **(c) On-demand now, scheduled monitor as a later wave** ✅ | Matches "surface when asked"; minimal first build |
| (a) On-demand pull only, forever | Fine for v1, but forecloses the L4 climb |
| (b) Continuous monitor from the start | Scheduler infra the pull path hasn't earned yet |

**Rationale:** on-demand pull is the most important capability and the minimal
build. The scheduled watcher is what L4's "no finding" eventually needs, but it is
a wave, not the foundation → filed as a **future GitHub Issue**.

### Q3 — Library storage unit → **scored candidate slices**

| Option | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **(b) Scored candidate slices (in/out vs. source), deduped at moment level** ✅ | Review *moments*, not sources; dedup where it matters |
| (a) Raw sources + provenance; extract on pull | Leaves tedious extraction in the loop every pull |
| (c) Finished cut clips | Over-commits storage; bakes in choices pre-review |

**Rationale:** the scored candidate-slice is the unit that actually kills the
bottleneck and lets deduplication work at the moment level — the thing a
two-celebrity beat needs.

### Q4 — Trim precision at handoff → **exact reviewed window** (amended 2026-09-20)

| Option | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **(a) Export the exact in/out ratified in Searcher's review UI** ✅ | The reviewed boundaries control the bytes Clipper transcribes, aligns, and renders |
| (b) Export a padded window + target in/out as metadata | Clipper currently renders the imported bytes as-is and does not apply the target metadata |

**Amendment rationale (2026-09-20):** the original padded-window choice assumed
RiceClipper would tighten around `clip.target_in/target_out`. Live code instead
imports the file unchanged, independently transcribes the complete imported audio,
and uses those clip-relative Whisper timings for captions and lyric alignment.
Padding therefore leaked beyond the reviewed boundaries and could shift lyric
alignment. The reviewer may now set any non-empty interval within the source, with
no artificial maximum duration. Invalid or out-of-source intervals are rejected
explicitly rather than clamped. Searcher exports exactly the saved interval and
rebuilds handoff transcript text from source words intersecting it; Clipper remains
the timing authority by transcribing those exact bytes. Manifest schema 1 remains:
its source bounds describe the exported interval and its clip-relative target is
`0` through the exported duration. Original candidate padding stays internal to
RiceSearcher's library as scoring/review context.

### Q5 — Approval gates on the road to L4 → **two gates now, converging toward auto-select**

| Option | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **(a) Review/select at Searcher→Clipper + existing pre-post gate** ✅ | Keeps taste in the loop before a render is spent |
| (b) Auto-select + render unattended; only the pre-post gate | Premature until scoring proves out |

**Rationale:** the posture is approval-queues-everywhere and never-auto-post, and
taste is the hardest part to automate. Keep the select step now; L4 does not require
deleting this gate, only making it one-click. Auto-select is a later dial-turn,
gated on the taste spike below.

## Trade-off Analysis

The central trade-off is **where framing intelligence lives**. Consolidating crop
into RiceClipper (Q1a) keeps framing in one pillar and keeps RiceSearcher's output a
clean time-bounded slice, at the cost of RiceClipper receiving un-cropped material
and owning the crop decision. The alternative — pushing crop upstream into
RiceSearcher — was rejected because it duplicates framing logic across two pillars
and fights the "RiceClipper is the visual pillar" principle.

The second trade-off is **context vs. boundary fidelity**. Candidate padding stays
in RiceSearcher's library so a reviewer can inspect context, but the handoff exports
only the exact reviewed interval (Q4 amended). This keeps moment-level review and
dedup while ensuring Clipper captions and lyric alignment operate on the selected
bytes alone.

## Consequences

**Easier**
- The daily "where do I even look / which part do I cut" bottleneck moves behind an
  on-demand pull with a scored, reviewable queue.
- Deduplication works at the moment level, protecting a finite beat from repeats.
- RiceClipper stays a focused renderer.

**Harder / to revisit**
- RiceClipper's penciled **Path 1 / Path 2 extraction engines formally retire out of
  RiceClipper's roadmap and into RiceSearcher.** This is a doc edit to be made
  against the real RiceClipper repo — not assumed here.
- Two gates add a review step; the intent is to collapse the first toward one-click
  as extraction earns trust.

## Resolved since ratification

- **Physical handoff format — resolved by 2026-09-20:** the live contract is a
  schema-1 filesystem pickup that mirrors the RiceClipper → RicePoster mechanism
  (one batch directory, manifest last, FIFO and stable batch identity). The
  amendment in Q4 makes each handed-off file exactly the reviewed interval; see
  `SPEC.md` §7 and the integration plan for the cross-repo details.

## Routed forward (this decision depends on these; not settled here)

- **Taste spike** — does LLM segment-scoring pick niche-clippable Person A/Person B
  moments well enough to trust? A prototype on real episodes, not a discussion.
  **Gates any move from Q5(a) → auto-select.**
- **Repo placement / bootstrap** — RiceSearcher is its own repo; bootstrap is a
  separate phase where reconciliation against the actual RiceClipper/RicePoster code
  happens.

## Explicitly out of scope of this ADR

Discovery source APIs, the scoring model, and the dedup mechanism — all downstream
spec, to be worked *after* the boundary is confirmed against live code.
